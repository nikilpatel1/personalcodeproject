About

I'm a sophomore at Babson College studying finance. I grew up in Newark, Delaware, and I've been starting things since I was a kid.

Where I started

I began streaming games around age 10. For years I had 10 to 20 viewers. The night I hit 100 concurrent viewers on Twitch is still the proudest moment from that stretch.

At 15, during the Epic Games v. Apple dispute, I turned gaming content into a profitable business. It grew to 40,000+ combined YouTube and Twitch followers and $35,000 in tournament winnings.

Family business

My family owned a restaurant in Philadelphia for ten years, and Newark Newsstand on Main Street in Newark, Delaware. I attended the Sanford School.

That background shaped what I want to do. In high school I proposed a leasing framework to help small businesses in my hometown compete with franchises. My long-term goal is to direct capital to the small businesses that are underserved today.

At Babson

I'm studying finance and recruiting for investment banking. Outside class I'm active in Scholars of Finance, Alpha Kappa Psi, Babson CODE, the SPEAR investment banking accelerator, and the Babson Finance Association. In 2026-27 I'm a peer mentor for Foundations of Management and Entrepreneurship.
